About

I grew up in Western Jutland, watching films with my grandparents on old VHS tapes. That led me to film school in Ebeltoft and to the film industry in Copenhagen, where I worked my way up from runner to production coordinator and 1st AD.

In 2024 I started Entrepreneurship & Design at KEA and stumbled into graphic design. My very first project was a poster for a made-up event on Christianshavn. I had just downloaded Adobe and had never designed anything. I've been hooked since.

Today I work with brand and visual identity, and I bring the film years with me: calm under pressure, used to deadlines, and always thinking about the story. I don't see myself as a finished designer, and I hope I never will.
