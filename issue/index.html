Front of bookEditor’s letter

Hi, I’m Bastian. Welcome to the first issue.

Since I was a small boy, I’ve been drawn to stories and visuals. Growing up in Western Jutland, I spent countless hours watching films with my grandparents on old VHS tapes. That led me to film school in Ebeltoft, and later to the film industry in Copenhagen, where I worked my way up from runner to production coordinator and 1st AD.

But film wasn’t the full picture for me. In 2024 I started Entrepreneurship & Design at KEA and stumbled into graphic design. From the first poster, I was hooked.

I don’t see myself as a finished designer, and I hope I never will. This issue is what I’ve made so far. Take your time, and turn the page.

The editorCopenhagen, 2026

Made to Play Longer

Sustainability was never part of the Palmes story. For my final project at KEA, I tried to find a way to take responsibility that still felt like Palmes, instead of bolting on a green layer.

Words & design by Bastian Fynboe
Made to Play Longer09
FeatureMade to Play Longer

Palmes is a Copenhagen tennis brand with a strong universe: red clay, court lines and a love of the game. Sustainability was never part of the story. As the founder put it in our interview, it simply wasn’t what Palmes was built around.

The question was how Palmes could take responsibility in a way that felt true to the brand.

What I found

I analysed how Aiayu, GANNI and Fichi communicate sustainability, and combined it with a survey and interviews with people in the target group. Everything pointed the same way.

To be believed, the message has to be simple, documentable and rooted in the brand’s own identity.

The concept

Made to Play Longer turns limits into the story. Each piece is made in a small, numbered run, often from deadstock fabric left over from earlier production. 140 pieces, because that is how much fabric there was.

The tone is concrete and a little dry. No leaves, no globes: navy, court green and a single clay accent.

14Vakkerbo

Identity, website and product photography for a new Danish studio for interior design, art and vintage.

Words & design by Bastian Fynboe
Vakkerbo15
FeatureVakkerbo

“Calm and personal, with room for the objects to speak.”

Vakkerbo is a new chapter from the founder of OYOY Living Design: interior design for homes and businesses, and a shop with one-off art and vintage pieces. It needed an identity and a website that felt calm and personal, with room for the objects to speak.

The website

I built the site as a custom Shopify theme. As you scroll, the large wordmark shrinks and settles into the header, and the menu fades in. The site runs in Danish and English, and the shop sells unique pieces, one of each.

Photography

I shot and edited the product photography for the shop: soft daylight, white lace and the pieces on their own.

Horses everywhere: a still from the film on the front page.
