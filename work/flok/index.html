04 Branding · Concept development

FLOK

A brand identity for FLOK, a community concept built on creativity, togetherness and collaboration.

ClientFLOK
Context1st semester exam, KEA
ServicesBranding, Visual identity, Concept development
MethodsSurveys, interviews, observations, the four design principles
The brief

The challenge was to capture FLOK's sense of togetherness in an identity that stands out on a busy street. A flock of swallows became the core idea: birds sitting on the letters, hand-drawn type and posters that invite people to come and eat together.
