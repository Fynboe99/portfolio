Folkets Drømme

An interactive installation in Folkets Park on Nørrebro that invited locals and visitors to share their dreams for the future.

ClientKEA / 48 Timer Festival
ContextTeam project, 48 Hours Festival
ServicesConcept development, Visual identity, Installation design
MethodsSurveys, interviews, observations
The idea

As part of the 48 Hours Festival, my team created “Folkets Drømme”. People wrote down their dreams and wishes, and we collected them in a large installation inspired by dreamcatchers, hung in the trees of the park. I worked on the concept and the visual identity, including the posters that called people in.
