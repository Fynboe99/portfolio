03 Visual identity · Packaging

Munkholm

A modernised identity for Munkholm, a Danish soap and skincare brand. The goal was to make it relevant to today's market while respecting its heritage.

ClientMunkholm
Context2nd semester exam, KEA
ServicesVisual identity, Branding, Packaging concepts
MethodsThe four design principles: contrast, repetition, alignment, proximity
The brief

Munkholm stands for quality, sustainability and simplicity, but the visual expression had fallen behind. Through research and design development I created an updated identity built on a quiet serif wordmark, warm natural tones and clear, honest labels.
