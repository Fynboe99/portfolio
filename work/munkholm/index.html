Munkholm

A modernised identity for Munkholm, a Danish soap and skincare brand. The goal was to make it relevant to today's market while respecting its heritage.

Munkholm
2nd semester exam, KEA
Visual identity, branding, packaging concepts
The four design principles: contrast, repetition, alignment, proximity

The brief

Munkholm stands for quality, sustainability and simplicity, but the visual expression had fallen behind. I built the new identity on a quiet, high-contrast serif wordmark, a warm apricot colour and labels that say plainly what is inside.
