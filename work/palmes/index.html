Palmes

Illustrations and print for Palmes Tennis Society, made during my internship in spring 2026. Above: a series of postcards for the shop.

ClientPalmes Tennis Society
ContextInternship, spring 2026
ServicesIllustration, print design, animation
ToolsIllustrator, Photoshop, After Effects
The work

Palmes has a strong universe of its own: green and cream, red clay, flowers and a lot of love for the game. My job was to draw inside that world, from postcards for the shop in Copenhagen to graphics for events and collaborations. Most of it starts as a pencil line, which I then clean up in Illustrator.
