Vakkerbo

Identity, website and product photography for Vakkerbo, a new Danish studio for interior design, art and vintage.

ClientVakkerbo
Year2026
ServicesVisual identity, custom Shopify theme, logo animation, product photography
Livevakkerbo.dk
The brief

Vakkerbo is a new chapter from the founder of OYOY Living Design: interior design for homes and businesses, and a shop with one-off art and vintage pieces. It needed an identity and a website that felt calm and personal, with room for the objects to speak.

The website

I built the site as a custom Shopify theme. As you scroll, the large wordmark shrinks and settles into the header, and the menu fades in. The site runs in Danish and English, and the shop sells unique pieces, one of each.

Photography

I shot and edited the product photography for the shop: soft daylight, white lace and the pieces on their own.
